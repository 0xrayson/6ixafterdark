// Philosophy

Good editing hides. Great editing is felt. Every cut out of 6ix Afterdark is built on the belief that attention isn't captured by accident — it's engineered, one deliberate frame at a time.

// What We Do

Built with
intent.

Three disciplines, one standard — nothing leaves the timeline until it earns the watch.

01

Brand Stories

A brand film has one job: make someone feel something before they've read a word of copy. Every story out of 6ix Afterdark is built shot-first and sound-second, because that's the order people actually experience it in.

02

Short-Form

The feed doesn't care about budget — it cares about the first second. Every short-form edit is cut to survive a thumb moving fast: clean, deliberate, and built to be watched all the way through, not scrolled past.

03

Wedding Films

A wedding happens once. The edit is what's left. Every film is treated less like a highlight reel and more like a scene — paced, scored and cut to be rewatched for years, not just shared once.

// About

The house
behind the name.

6ix Afterdark is built and directed by Temitope Sonaike, an editor working out of Lagos with an engineer's eye for detail — trained first in Computer Engineering, sharpened since on the timeline.

The name is literal. The work happens after dark, in the hours reserved for the fourth pass nobody asked for: the frame trimmed by two units, the sound that sits half a beat early, the difference most people never consciously notice, but always feel.
